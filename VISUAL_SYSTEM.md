# CarbonSense Visual System

## Design direction

CarbonSense is styled as a **trusted climate intelligence lab**: precise rather than playful, calm rather than generic eco-wellness, and visibly data-native without presenting estimates as verified measurements. The repeated brand motif is **two lenses over one carbon profile**, expressed through signal rings, measurement grids, mono data labels, and controlled comparison panels.

| System element | Applied direction |
|---|---|
| Typography | Manrope for display hierarchy, DM Sans for readable interface text, and DM Mono for data labels, values, and traceable model context. |
| Color | Deep carbon green and mint form the primary field; an exact warm amber is reserved for administrator context and status emphasis. |
| Surface | High-clarity translucent cards with restrained elevation, fine green borders, and a shared soft mint background. |
| Hierarchy | Signal labels, numbered tools, scoped data-boundary callouts, and metric typography consistently establish scan order. |
| Motion | Brief transform/color transitions use the shared snappy easing and remain disabled under reduced-motion preferences. |

## Desktop review

Desktop captures of the landing, Explore, Plan, and Admin routes confirmed that the new shell, data-lens hero, signal frames, metric cards, filter panel, and explicit aggregate empty states render without clipping. The revised administrator surface keeps amber confined to its governance role while retaining the shared CarbonSense data language.

## Mobile review

Mobile captures of the landing, Optimizer, Reports, and Admin routes confirmed that the redesigned content stack remains readable at 390 px. The mobile menu preserves brand recognition without overcrowding the header; the planner controls remain comfortably tappable; report health and model-boundary panels remain visibly distinct; and the administrator metrics, empty states, filters, and organization summaries stack without horizontal overflow.

Additional route-specific validation confirmed that the Optimizer and Report Center retain their full desktop hierarchy, including the planner signal frame, range controls, report metadata, health status, and printable-summary action. The protected Explore hub also retains its signal frame, recommended next move, tool tabs, and data cards in a single readable mobile column.
