# CarbonSense product-review evidence

## Observed running experience

- The current preview is running and presents a polished, consistent climate-intelligence workspace with the Explore, Plan, Insights, Progress, Docs, and Admin navigation.
- The public landing page has a strong dark hero, a clear three-step workflow, and a card-based instrument directory.
- The main workspace hubs currently serve primarily as elegant route directories. Their zero-data states do not yet display a compact analytical artifact, readiness signal, or individualized next decision.
- Explore separates AI prediction and transparent baseline; Plan surfaces optimizer, What-if, and net-zero routes; Insights surfaces forecast and history; Progress surfaces CarbonQuest and contributors.
- Current desktop auth screens retain the lighter `C` tile while the workspace uses the concentric signal mark, creating a brand-consistency opportunity.

## Supplied-screen observations

- The detailed Explore flow demonstrates an approachable, four-step questionnaire with progress indication, sliders, compact segmented options, visual choice cards, and a focused primary action.
- The supplied planning experience provides useful controls for a roadmap goal, percentage shortcuts, time horizon, and a clear empty state. It offers a strong interaction pattern but uses more generic emoji-driven decoration and broader “AI-powered” claims than the current evidence-led style should retain.
- The strongest reusable pattern is a guided decision flow: one question or plan choice at a time, visible progress, meaningful defaults, and a concrete next action.
- Recommended improvements must retain CarbonSense’s existing safeguards: do not imply verified reductions before evidence exists, keep forecasts explicitly uncertainty-bounded, and never reveal individual member history in organization insights.
