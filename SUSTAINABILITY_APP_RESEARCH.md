# Sustainability-app design research

**Research date:** 19 August 2026

## Reviewed patterns

| Product | Publicly described pattern | CarbonSense implication |
|---|---|---|
| [AWorld](https://www.aworld.org/blog/aworld-news/app-to-track-your-carbon-footprint-discover-aworld-for-act-now/) | Daily action logging, multi-metric savings, learning, challenges, XP/levels, team rankings, and exportable outcome reporting | Keep CarbonSense’s verified activity/gamification foundation, but foreground one next action, visible progress, and an evidence-aware team/organization view. |
| [Commons](https://www.thecommons.earth/) | Weekly impact check-in, goals tied to financial behaviour, sustainable-brand discovery, rewards, and approachable progress framing | Make the weekly carbon signal and a single practical action more prominent; use a calmer, more editorial presentation rather than overwhelming dashboards. |
| [Joro summary](https://knowesg.com/solutions/consumer-sustainability-apps/joro) | Personal emissions insights, real-time spend-based tracking, climate action plans, financial-data integration, community goals, and education | Combine a contextual insight with an explicit next step; preserve CarbonSense’s manual survey and consent boundaries rather than adding financial ingestion by default. |

## Design principles selected for CarbonSense

CarbonSense should retain its distinct climate-intelligence focus rather than copy either product. The next iteration should combine: a calm editorial headline system, a low-saturation mineral-and-forest palette with one energetic action accent, a “this week” impact focus, a single next-best action, clear category drivers, transparent confidence/method access, and progressively disclosed technical information.

No claims, visual assets, customer reviews, or proprietary content from the reviewed products will be copied into CarbonSense.

The publicly indexed Klima source was unavailable in this research pass because its page was blocked by Cloudflare, so no design decision relies on it.
