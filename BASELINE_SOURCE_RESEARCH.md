# Transparent Baseline Source Scope

## Verified source roles

The **DESNZ 2026 UK government conversion factors** are the primary direct-factor source for the calculator’s UK electricity, natural-gas, and road-travel activity inputs. The publisher states that these factors support greenhouse-gas calculations from activity data such as purchased electricity in kWh and distance travelled. They are suitable for UK operations and international organisations reporting on UK operations, so CarbonSense must retain its UK-reference qualifier. [1]

The calculator’s food values are global-average life-cycle estimates attributed to **Poore and Nemecek (2018)**. The Our World in Data presentation identifies the source as a 38,700-farm, 119-country, 40-product dataset and cautions that food footprints can vary substantially by production location and practice. CarbonSense must label these as global averages, rather than local dietary measurements. [2]

The **GHG Protocol** source is appropriate as calculation-framework context because it documents the breadth and reporting use of Defra/DECC conversion factors. It is not used as the numeric origin of a separate displayed factor. [3]

The **US EPA WARM** source is appropriate as a method reference for material-specific waste pathways. It should not support a single generic landfill or recycling number unless the calculator first gathers material and treatment-route data. [4]
