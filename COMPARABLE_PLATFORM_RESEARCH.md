# Comparable sustainability platform research

## Initial website review

| Platform | Website | Public positioning | Pattern relevant to CarbonSense |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commons | [thecommons.earth](https://www.thecommons.earth/) | “Save Money, Save the Planet”; connects values, spending, goals, brand ratings, rewards, and collective challenges. | Use one memorable outcome headline, a highly visible primary app/action CTA, and a product-interface preview that makes the benefit tangible. CarbonSense can adapt the clarity, not the financial-spend tracking model. |
| AWorld | [aworld.org](https://www.aworld.org/) | Enterprise engagement and education platform with separate products, audience types, demo/start actions, case studies, and gamification/engagement content. | Clearly distinguish individual action support from organization-level engagement. CarbonSense can surface its individual/organization pathways without mixing their permissions or data. |
| Watershed | [watershed.com](https://watershed.com/) | Enterprise sustainability AI platform organized around a clear **Measure → Report → Act** progression, with tour/demo calls to action and methodology/data-lineage emphasis. | CarbonSense should use a smaller-scale **Measure → Compare → Act** narrative. The idea of showing each stage before users enter the workflow fits the homepage well. |
| Greenly | [greenly.earth](https://greenly.earth/) | Carbon-management platform that leads with climate strategy, AI assistance, reporting/decarbonization products, and a visible demo action. | Use one primary action and clear functional product categories. Avoid copying enterprise client-count or compliance claims that CarbonSense cannot substantiate. |

## Early homepage takeaway

Commons makes the first screen about a simple human benefit and shows a product preview immediately. AWorld makes audience and product pathways explicit. CarbonSense should combine those lessons: a concise human result (“understand your estimate and next step”), an immediate workflow preview, and a clear branch between personal measurement and organization-provided guidance.

Watershed offers the best structural reference for the CarbonSense homepage because it turns a complex product into a simple sequence. Greenly reinforces the value of a primary action and a clearly categorized solution set. CarbonSense should adapt these navigation and hierarchy ideas while retaining its own scientific-boundary language and individual-first workflow.

## Additional public calculator references

| Calculator | Website | Confirmed scope | CarbonSense takeaway |
|---|---|---|---|
| U.S. EPA Household Carbon Footprint Calculator | [epa.gov/ghgemissions/carbon-footprint-calculator](https://www.epa.gov/ghgemissions/carbon-footprint-calculator) | Household home energy, transportation, and waste; asks for household/ZIP information, provides an assumptions/references page, and frames planned actions as reductions. | Offer a clear assumptions/methodology route and collect activity details only where the user can supply them. CarbonSense’s transparent baseline follows the same principle but must keep its UK-factor boundary visible. |
| WWF Footprint Calculator | [footprint.wwf.org.uk](https://footprint.wwf.org.uk/) | A public UK footprint-calculator entry point. The live page did not render usable content in the review browser session. | Retain this as a reference link, but do not infer its current interaction design without a successful render. |
| The Nature Conservancy Calculator | [nature.org carbon calculator](https://www.nature.org/en-us/get-involved/how-to-help/carbon-footprint-calculator/) | Public individual calculator framed around learning a footprint and actions to reduce it. | A helpful reference for simple educational framing: calculate first, then offer action. |
| Global Footprint Network Calculator | [footprintcalculator.org](https://www.footprintcalculator.org/) | Public ecological-footprint calculator entry point. The page did not render usable content in the review browser session. | It is better treated as an ecological-footprint reference rather than evidence for a carbon-only result flow. |
